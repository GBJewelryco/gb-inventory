Backup

Your inventory saves automatically to your GitHub (encrypted with the passcode), so it's the same on every device. Downloading a backup once a week is still a good idea, so you always have your own copy.

Spreadsheet (CSV)

Export to open in Excel or Google Sheets, or load your current stock all at once. Import has one row per size: sku, name, category, karat, color, cost_type, size, qty, unit_cost, price, low. Rows for a SKU or size that already exists update it; qty sets the count on hand (new units are added at unit_cost).

Lists & gold settings

Gold purity used in cost math (%)

Gold cost per gram = spot price ÷ 31.1035 × purity. Raise a number a little if your supplier charges for alloy or loss.

Passcode & GitHub key
