Backup

Your inventory saves automatically to your GitHub (encrypted with the passcode), so it's the same on every device. Downloading a backup once a week is still a good idea, so you always have your own copy.

Spreadsheet (CSV)

Export your inventory to open in Excel or Google Sheets, or import many items at once. Import columns: sku, name, category, metal, stone, size, qty, low, cost, price, notes. Rows with a SKU that already exists update that item.
